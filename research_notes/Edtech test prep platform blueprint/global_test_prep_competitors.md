# Global commercial test-prep platforms (IELTS, GRE, PTE, TOEFL, DET) as of Nov 2026

Note on method: research was shallow-to-moderate (about 25 tool calls). Prices are USD unless stated, and "seen" dates come from the cited aggregator pages (mostly Oct 21 - Nov 5, 2026), NOT from my own visits to vendor pages unless stated. Aggregator/affiliate review sites (katalystprep, edisonos, learnenglish.life) have commercial incentives; EdisonOS is a competitor of Magoosh.

## 1. What does a paying user get at each price tier, and what is free (GRE + IELTS focus)?

### Takeaway
GRE prep is a tiered ladder: free official tests and freemium YouTube/blog creators at the bottom, $12-$180 self-paced subscriptions (GregMat, Magoosh, PrepScholar), $450-$600 premium self-paced (Target Test Prep, Manhattan Prep, Kaplan), then $900-$5,000 live classes and tutoring with score guarantees. IELTS prep is split between free creators (IELTS Liz), official free-with-booking tools (British Council), and AI-scoring subscriptions in the $20-$30/month range.

### Cited Findings

- Magoosh: 7-day free trial; GRE Premium $149 (1 month, 6 practice tests, score-increase guarantee with 4 conditions), $179 (6 months), Premium + Admissions $219 (6 months). Magoosh's own page confirms a 7-day money-back guarantee, a score improvement guarantee, 1,000+ vocab flashcards, a free GRE practice test, daily study plans/schedules and a free practice lesson — [Magoosh GRE](https://magoosh.com/gre/). A separate piece dated Oct 30, 2026 lists the same $149/$179/$219 — [EdisonOS](https://www.edisonos.com/alternatives/magoosh-pricing). Magoosh also sells TOEFL, GMAT and (per my background knowledge, unverified here) IELTS; no TOEFL/GMAT/IELTS prices were found.
- Target Test Prep (TTP): $179/mo (regular ~$229); 4 months $449 (reg. $549); 6 months $499 (reg. $599); 6 months + live classes $1,299 (reg. $1,599); private tutoring 5 hrs $1,700; score-increase guarantee on subscription tiers.

### Inferences
- The price anchors for a new GRE entrant: sub-$20/mo (GregMat), ~$150-$180 (Magoosh), ~$450-$600 (TTP/Manhattan/Kaplan self-paced), $900+ with live teaching. A $15-30/month IELTS+GRE bundle with AI scoring would sit in the cheap-subscription band where AI IELTS apps already compete.
- Score-increase guarantees are near-universal at $449+ and tie the buyer to usage conditions (Magoosh lists 4 conditions, PrepScholar 6), which acts as a retention and refund-control device.

### Gaps
- Prep (prepedu) actual prices; IELTS Advantage and IELTS Simon products/prices; Magoosh IELTS/TOEFL prices; IDP's own prep app (IELTS Prep app / IDP "Ready" tools) contents and pricing; DET free practice details; Cambridge's IELTS official prep beyond Write & Improve; TOEFL (ETS TOEFL Essentials/iBT Official Prep) not researched at all.

## 2. Which monetization patterns are most commercially successful (revenue/user figures)?

### Takeaway
Public financials are scarce. Observable patterns: test owners (ETS, Pearson, Duolingo, British Council) use prep as a funnel/add-on to the exam fee (free or bundled), independents use subscriptions with guarantees, and funded Asia startups (Prep) pair freemium AI practice with credits tiers. B2B/institution sales exist (Magoosh bulk pricing from ten accounts).

### Cited Findings
- Prep Edu (prepedu): US$7M Series A announced 8 Jan 2026 (investors: Cercano Management Asia, Northstar Ventures, Saison Capital); claims 1M+ learners across 21 countries, 126 exclusive test sets, 100+ certified instructors/former examiners; targets IELTS and TOEIC. Revenue not disclosed; issuer-written release — [Newswav / Media OutReach](https://newswav.com/article/prep-edu-raises-7m-usd-to-scale-its-ai-powered-ielts-learning-platform-A2601_altaUb).
- Magoosh offers bulk pricing for institutes starting at ten accounts, with no custom mock uploads, no proctoring, and Magoosh-branded UX (from a competitor's article, so treat with caution) — [EdisonOS reviews](https://www.edisonos.com/alternatives/magoosh-reviews).
- British Council uses IELTS Ready Premium as a free perk for booking its IELTS test (exam-fee funnel) — [British Council TH](https://www.britishcouncil.or.th/en/ielts-ready-premium).
- Pearson monetizes official practice via per-test purchases and good-better-best bundles (Bronze/Silver/Gold) with heavy "was" price anchoring (Gold $119.99 vs $237.94 struck-through) — [Pearson PTE](https://www.pearsonpte.com/preparation/official-practice-tests).
- DET: sold as a low-fee test ($70) with free official practice, i.e. the test itself is the product — [Cialfo](https://www.cialfo.co/blog/the-complete-guide-to-the-duolingo-english-test-2026).
- Affiliate: the review ecosystem (Katalyst Prep, EdisonOS, bloggersideas, etc.) heavily covers these products; whether they use affiliate links was not verified.

### Inferences
- Freemium + low monthly price + creator-led distribution (GregMat, IELTS Liz) appears to be the main independent challenger model; premium incumbents rely on guarantees and live tutoring for high ticket sizes. No data found to say which earns most.

### Gaps
- No revenue, subscriber, or conversion figures found for Magoosh (acquired by Kaplan-era history not verified; I did not confirm current ownership), GregMat, TTP, Kaplan, Princeton Review, E2Language, IELTS Liz. Crunchbase/press revenue searches were not done. Any "recent acquisitions/shutdowns" (e.g. Kaplan/Princeton Review/Manhattan Prep ownership changes) were NOT researched; flag as unchecked.

## 3. How do they do AI evaluation of IELTS writing/speaking and GRE essay, and how are scores presented?

### Takeaway
GRE essays: ETS uses e-rater automated scoring in ScoreItNow. IELTS: nearly all apps use LLMs mapped to the four public band descriptors, return per-criterion bands plus highlighted passages, and disclaim that scores are indicators. Published accuracy evidence is thin and sometimes contradictory; IDP says official Writing/Speaking are human-marked and advises against LLM grading.

### Cited Findings
- ETS ScoreItNow: e-rater immediate scoring, diagnostic feedback — [ScoreItNow](https://scoreitnow.dxrgroup.com/scoreitnow).
- Prep: AI feedback on pronunciation, coherence, task response, grammatical accuracy; goal-based study plans; says its scores are for tracking progress, not predicting exact test score — [Newswav](https://newswav.com/article/prep-edu-raises-7m-usd-to-scale-its-ai-powered-ielts-learning-platform-A2601_altaUb); [Prep pricing](https://prepedu.com/en/ielts/lab/pricing).
- PrepEx: feedback tied to public band descriptors (task achievement, coherence/cohesion, lexical resource, grammar, fluency, pronunciation); compares to Band 5/7/9 samples; no validation details; unsourced headline claim "3 bands average improvement" — [PrepEx](https://www.prepex.ai/ielts/pricing).
- IELTS International (third-party, not affiliated with IELTS owners despite "official" wording): LLM fine-tuned on band descriptors and "thousands" of real responses; transcribes speaking audio then scores 4 criteria; claims writing within 0.5 band of examiners; admits weakness with unconventional writing, accents, fast speech; no methodology published — [IELTS International](https://www.ielts.international/pa/ielts-ai-feedback).
- Accuracy data points: Cathoven claims 98% within 0.5 band on one page but 75-80% for AI tools generally on another; Band Score Checker's own small test: 79% of Task 2 scores and 70% of Task 1 within half a band, and band 8.5 essays all scored 7; an open Hugging Face model hit within-0.5 only 50.3% of the time (noisy labels); AI is strongest on vocabulary/grammar and weakest on argument/Task Response — [AI Tools Bakery](https://aitoolsbakery.com/?p=19156). IDP says official IELTS Writing and Speaking are human-marked and advises against LLM grading (same source).
- Pearson PTE: AI Practice gives instant scoring based on official scoring traits; the real PTE is machine-scored (inference from "same scoring engine" wording on the Scored Practice Test) — [Pearson PTE](https://www.pearsonpte.com/preparation/official-practice-tests).
- DET: AI-scored test — [Cialfo](https://www.cialfo.co/blog/the-complete-guide-to-the-duolingo-english-test-2026).
- British Council: AI feedback optional on mocks, plus an estimated band on the Mini Mock — [British Council TH](https://www.britishcouncil.or.th/en/ielts-ready-premium).

### Inferences
- Common presentation pattern: overall band + 4 criterion bands + inline error highlights + sample answer comparison + disclaimer. A credible entrant should publish its own validation (agreement with certified examiner marks within 0.5 band) since competitors mostly don't, which is a differentiator.

### Gaps
- Magoosh/GregMat/TTP GRE essay grading (whether human, AI, or none) was not retrieved; GregMat page review did not mention an AI essay grader. PrepScholar offers "essay grading" with lifetime plan, method unknown.

## 4. Common UX patterns in mock-test engines and dashboards

### Takeaway
Evidence retrieved is feature-list level, not screenshots. Reported patterns: timed, section-based simulations of the real interface; full score report; study plans; progress dashboards; sample-answer comparison; AI tutor chat.

### Cited Findings
- Pearson Scored Practice Test replicates the real test (2 hours, same engine) and produces a score report by skill; multiple versions — [Pearson PTE](https://www.pearsonpte.com/preparation/official-practice-tests).
- ScoreItNow offers a "Test Experience" timed online mode vs untimed "Practice" mode — [ScoreItNow](https://scoreitnow.dxrgroup.com/scoreitnow).
- British Council Premium: personalised dashboard with study recommendations and progress tracking; mock tests with scores and model answers; shorter 25-minute express mock — [British Council TH](https://www.britishcouncil.or.th/en/ielts-ready-premium).
- Prep: goal/schedule/baseline-based study planning; mock tests with strict timing; instant AI band scores; free sign-in needed to save progress — [Newswav](https://newswav.com/article/prep-edu-raises-7m-usd-to-scale-its-ai-powered-ielts-learning-platform-A2601_altaUb); [Prep pricing](https://prepedu.com/en/ielts/lab/pricing).
- PrepEx: section-timed full tests, 15-20 minute quick diagnostics with band reports, spaced-repetition grammar drills, AI coach with daily message limit on free tier — [PrepEx](https://www.prepex.ai/ielts/pricing).
- DET: adaptive test (difficulty adjusts in real time) — [Cialfo](https://www.cialfo.co/blog/the-complete-guide-to-the-duolingo-english-test-2026). Real GRE is section-adaptive (background knowledge; not sourced here).

### Gaps
- No screenshots/UX walkthroughs of timer, flag-for-review, review screen, navigation for any engine (ETS POWERPREP, Magoosh, TTP). Would require product trials or YouTube walkthroughs.

## 6. Common user complaints a new entrant can exploit

### Takeaway
Little first-hand complaint data was retrievable (Trustpilot/Reddit pages surfaced but not read). Findings are second-hand.

### Cited Findings
- Magoosh: some reviewers say questions are harder than the real exam, ambiguous wording, differences from official style; institutions lack proprietary content upload, proctoring, and deep admin reporting; Trustpilot 4.8/5 across 600+ reviews as cited by a competitor — [EdisonOS reviews](https://www.edisonos.com/alternatives/magoosh-reviews). Trustpilot pages exist at trustpilot.com/review/magoosh.com but were not read.
- AI apps: unclear/hidden pricing (in-app prices differing from store descriptions for Stimuler, IELTSpeaking), most publish no accuracy evidence, platform fragmentation (web-only, iPhone-only), generous scoring bias at the high end — [AI Tools Bakery](https://aitoolsbakery.com/?p=19156).
- British Council Premium is gated behind booking with the British Council; Write & Improve is writing-only — same source.
- Guarantees have multiple conditions (Magoosh 4, PrepScholar 6) — [Katalyst Prep](https://katalystprep.com/gre/courses/).

### Inferences
- Opportunities: (1) validated and transparent AI scoring with examiner-agreement stats; (2) one subscription covering all four IELTS skills + GRE with full mocks and cross-device apps; (3) transparent pricing and simple guarantee; (4) interface-faithful test engine.

### Gaps
- No Reddit r/GRE or r/IELTS threads read; no Trustpilot content for Kaplan, Princeton Review, TTP, Manhattan, GregMat, E2Language.

### Cited Findings
