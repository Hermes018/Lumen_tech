# South Asian and Bangladeshi Ed-tech and Study-Abroad Platforms (as of Nov 2026)

Scope note: about 20 tool calls were used. Coverage is uneven. 10 Minute School, Mentors', Bohubrihi, Ostad, Shikho, Programming Hero, PhysicsWallah, Unacademy/upGrad, BYJU'S, Leverage Edu and Yocket have findings. ## 1. 10 Minute School (10MS): funding, users, products, IELTS offering, offline English Centre

### Takeaway
10MS grew from a free Facebook page (2014) and YouTube channel into Bangladesh's largest ed-tech through free-content funnels. It monetizes with paid batches, skills courses, IELTS programmes and offline English Centres. Its 2024 setbacks were a cancelled government investment and a halt of live, revision and offline classes. Public numbers are patchy and partly from a low-quality aggregator.

### Cited Findings
- Founder Ayman Sadiq launched 10MS in mid-2014 as a free Facebook page, added YouTube in 2015, and launched the website in May 2015. The company was bootstrapped on tutoring and YouTube ad revenue until 2021. The source is an AI-generated wiki with flagged citation problems, so treat as medium confidence — [Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/10_Minute_School)
- Funding per the same aggregator: $2M seed led by Sequoia (Surge) in Jan 2022, then a BDT 61 crore (about $5.5M) pre-Series A led by Conjunction Capital in Oct 2023. Totals are inconsistent in the source: about $7.5M versus Tk 78 crore — [Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/10_Minute_School)
- The same source claims 6.7M app downloads, 3.2M monthly active learners (2023), 52% of users rural or semi-urban, 34% female, and 3.34M YouTube subscribers (June 2025). Unverified against primary sources — [Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/10_Minute_School)
- Freemium model per the same source. Free content on YouTube and the app. Paid batches priced at about BDT 500-2,000 per course (weakly sourced). Also YouTube ads and corporate/telecom sponsorships, an e-book store, and 60+ Skills programmes plus 30+ free courses — [Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/10_Minute_School)
- Startup Bangladesh (government fund) cancelled a Tk 5 crore investment proposal on 16 July 2024. 10MS halted all live, revision and offline English Centre classes indefinitely from 3 Aug 2024, announced on Facebook. The article gives no official reason, but notes CEO Sadiq had posted in solidarity with quota protesters — [TBS News](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/908686)
- Grokipedia also reports that SBK Tech Ventures defaulted on its pledges to portfolio startups including 10MS (Oct 2025). This is a single weak source — [Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/10_Minute_School)
- IELTS Course (recorded). Specs: 50 hours, 54 videos (25 lectures), 38 lecture sheets, 10 Reading and 10 Listening mock tests with instant results in a computer-delivered style, 600+ vocabulary words, weekly live Zoom doubt-solving, a Facebook support group, a free hardcopy book "ঘরে বসে IELTS প্রস্তুতি", lifetime validity. Price not shown on the page — [10MS IELTS Course](https://10minuteschool.com/en/product/ielts-course)
- IELTS Live Batch. Specs: 12 weeks, 36 live classes (50 min lecture + 30 min Q&A), 12 months validity, 8 Reading + 8 Listening practice tests, 2 full mocks, 1:1 mentor consultations, an exclusive book, a "24/7" Facebook support group. Price not shown — [10MS IELTS Live Batch](https://10minuteschool.com/en/product/ielts-live-batch)
- IELTS Programme (the blended/English Centre version). Specs: 3 months live plus 3 months ancillary, 31 live classes (Listening 8, Reading 8, Writing 11, Speaking 4), three weekly batch patterns, Cambridge and IELTS Official materials, 12 partial mocks, 12 speaking mocks, 12 full mocks, 12 solve classes, 6-month Language Club, 1:1 speaking pods, progress tracking, and free 1:1 study-abroad support. Registration is at an English Centre or online — [10MS IELTS Programme](https://10minuteschool.com/en/product/ielts-programme/)
- English Centre brochure (2024 PDF). IELTS Regular is BDT 15,000 (3 months, 3 days a week, 2 hours each). Spoken English Junior levels 1-3 are BDT 15,000 each, and the full 9-month programme is BDT 40,000. All fees include 15% VAT. Extras: unlimited mocks, 1:1 speaking tests, 1,000+ practice materials, free repeat if the target score is missed — [10MS English Centre brochure](https://cdn.10minuteschool.com/images/Brochure_10MS_English_Centre_1715256964842.pdf)
- Refund policy: 48-hour request window via hotline 16910; non-refundable if more than 5 premium videos are watched or a certificate is earned; e-books are non-refundable; refunds are returned to the original payment medium in 7-14 working days. bKash/Nagad/cards are not named on the page — [10MS Refund Policy](https://10minuteschool.com/refund-policy)
- 10MS also has a "Study Abroad Complete Guideline" product, a kids' English product, and HSC online batches with product pages (e.g., HSC 27 ICT) — [search result list](https://10minuteschool.com/en/product/study-abroad-complete-guideline)

### Inferences
- Funnel: free YouTube/Facebook, then a free course or app, then a paid recorded course (BDT thousands), then a live batch, then the offline English Centre (BDT 15,000 per 3-month course). Each step has a clear price ladder.
- The IELTS offer is deliberately tiered. Recorded (self-paced, cheapest), Live Batch (12 weeks), and Programme/Centre (blended, most expensive). Support via a Facebook group and Zoom doubt sessions is the cheap way to scale human help.
- Offline centres and live classes were suspended in Aug 2024 amid a political and funding shock. This shows key-person and political risk.

### Gaps
- Online IELTS course prices on 10MS pages are not shown in fetched text (probably rendered dynamically). Not found.
- No primary-source revenue, profit or current (2026) user numbers. The Grokipedia figures are unverified.
- Tech stack, payment gateway list (bKash/Nagad/SSLCommerz), TikTok usage, gamification and user roles were not confirmed for 10MS.
- Status of English Centres after the Aug 2024 halt was not checked. Whether classes resumed is unknown from this research.

## 2. Other Bangladeshi platforms: Shikho, Bohubrihi, Ostad, Programming Hero, IELTS coaching brands (Mentors')

### Takeaway
Shikho serves school and admission-test students on a subscription app. Bohubrihi (owned by Shikho) uses tiered subscriptions of BDT 12,000-24,000 including GRE/IELTS prep. Ostad runs live, task-based skills courses at about Tk 6,000 per course. Offline IELTS brands such as Mentors' charge BDT 13,800 for Regular and BDT 9,500 for Crash.

### Cited Findings
- Shikho: seed round of $4M plus $1.3M (Aug 2021), total $5.3M, led by Wavemaker Partners (articles dated 29 Mar 2022). 350,000+ users across all 64 districts; paid subscribers average 68 min a day. Features: animated Bangla video lessons, question bank with solutions, performance analytics, smart notes, live classes. Launched Nov 2020 for SSC/HSC and admission prep. Subscription prices are not given — [The Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/node/2993191)
- Shikho acquired Bohubrihi (relaunched 2023) — [Future Startup](https://futurestartup.com/2023/05/11/bohubrihi-relaunche-shikhos-acquisition-of-bohubrihi/) (title only; content not fetched) and the Daily Star article above
- Bohubrihi subscription (Oct 2023): 6 months BDT 12,000 (all Foundation courses and Career Tracks); 1 year BDT 18,000 (was 24,000) plus 6 personal live sessions; 2 years BDT 24,000 plus 16 personal sessions. 40+ paid Foundation courses (software, data science, business, marketing, productivity, GRE and IELTS prep) and 5 Career Tracks of 4-6 months — [Future Startup](https://futurestartup.com/2023/10/25/bohubrihi-subscription/)
- Ostad (founded Oct 2020 by five KUET graduates): live, task-based courses; average Tk 6,000 per course; 28 courses; about 400-450 students per month; 6,000+ completed, 3,500 reportedly placed, about 60 hiring companies; live classes handle 500 concurrent students (target 1,000). Funding: $25k angel, $12k angel (Dec 2021), $90k from SBK/Miyako/SOSV (Feb 2022), $150k from SOSV (Dec 2022). Article dated 19 Mar 2023 — [Prothom Alo English](https://en.prothomalo.com/youth/education/wlc3y5n7xq)
- Programming Hero (Jhankar Mahbub; July 2021 article): web development course Tk 5,500 (Tk 1,000 pandemic discount), about 5,500 enrolled, only 33% completed, app downloaded about 1M times with 4.8 stars, support responses within 24 hours, interview-prep class, mobile game-style app. No revenue or funding given — [The Business Standard](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/271810)
- Mentors' Education (British Council test centre partner) on-campus fees: IELTS Regular BDT 13,800 (3.5 months, 20 mocks); IELTS Crash BDT 9,500 (1.5 months, 10 mocks); IELTS Basic Package BDT 19,900; PTE BDT 11,903; SAT BDT 13,973; Spoken English BDT 7,556; GED BDT 60,000. Fees as shown Nov 2026, with discounts expiring 15 Nov 2026. Online pricing not listed — [Mentors' course fees](https://www.mentors.com.bd/course-fee)
- Mentors' is also an IELTS exam registration channel (multiple "British Council, Mentors Education" test centres) — [IELTS.org test centres](https://ielts.org/test-centres/british-council-mentors-education-banani)

### Inferences
- Benchmark IELTS price band in Dhaka for offline: BDT 9,500 (crash) to 15,000 (3 months) plus a bundle ceiling near BDT 20,000. Recorded online IELTS would sensibly price below this, but no verified online price was found.
- Subscription (Bohubrihi) and single-course purchase coexist. Skills courses cluster at Tk 5,500-6,000.
- Low completion (33% at Programming Hero) is a recurring failure mode for recorded courses. Ostad explicitly pushes live learning to reduce dropout.

### Gaps
- Udvash-Unmesh online, Interactive Cares, Ghoori Learning, Saifurs: not researched (no fetched sources).
- Shikho's subscription prices, 2025-2026 user numbers and financial position were not found. Figures are 2022.
- Ostad and Programming Hero data are 2021-2023, so likely outdated for late 2026.
- British Council Bangladesh's own course pricing was not retrieved.
- Payment methods (bKash, Nagad, Rocket, SSLCommerz), tech stack and job postings for all these platforms were not found.

## 3. India: PhysicsWallah, Unacademy, upGrad, Scaler, BYJU'S, and what went wrong

### Takeaway
The India winners are hybrid online + offline operators (PhysicsWallah). Pandemic-era venture-funded growth (BYJU'S, Unacademy) collapsed or sold at a steep discount. Lesson: test prep has high customer acquisition cost and poor repeat purchase, and debt-fuelled acquisition is fatal.

### Cited Findings
- PhysicsWallah FY25 (DRHP data): operating revenue Rs 2,887 crore (+49% from Rs 1,941 crore); online coaching about Rs 1,404 crore, offline about Rs 1,352 crore (about 47% of revenue); offline ARPU Rs 40,405; positive EBITDA Rs 192.5 crore; 5,096 faculty. The source itself notes segment figures do not reconcile — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/fintrackr/physicswallah-spent-rs-1426-cr-on-salaries-in-fy25-offline-arpu-stood-at-rs-40405-10059672)
- PhysicsWallah IPO: Rs 3,820 crore DRHP; later listed at a 33.94% premium (Rs 146). Plans Rs 460 crore of IPO proceeds for offline expansion — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/start-up/news/physicswallah-to-back-offline-push-with-460-cr-from-ipo-proceeds); [5paisa](https://www.5paisa.com/index.php/news/physicswallah-ipo-listing) (headlines from search results; not fetched)
- Unacademy was sold to upGrad for about $200M, all in upGrad shares, completed 1 Sep 2026. That is about 94% below its $3.4B 2021 peak. Unacademy FY25 revenue fell 16% to Rs 702 crore, net loss Rs 435 crore. upGrad FY25 revenue Rs 1,569 crore, loss Rs 274 crore. Article's lesson: "education isn't software"; test prep lacks recurring customers and CAC stays high — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/unacademys-sale-to-upgrad-is-the-final-act-of-indias-edtech-boom-553343-2026-09-04)
- BYJU'S: $22B peak valuation (2022), roughly $3B Aakash acquisition, $1.2B term loan (Nov 2021), delayed audits and auditor resignations, alleged $500M+ diverted funds, insolvency proceedings from July 2024. In May 2026 a Singapore court sentenced founder Byju Raveendran to six months in jail for non-compliance with asset orders — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/from-22-billion-peak-to-debt-traps-and-lawsuits-how-byjus-empire-collapsed-533611-2026-05-28)
- Scaler: headline reports FY25 revenue declined to Rs 363 crore with losses cut by 98%. Only the headline was seen — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/fintrackr/scaler-revenue-declines-to-rs-363-cr-in-fy25-cuts-losses-by-98-10957481)

### Inferences
- Lessons for a Dhaka launch: avoid debt-funded expansion, keep unit economics (CAC vs. price) positive, and favour a hybrid model with human-led live support (PW's offline arm is about half of revenue).
- Aggressive sales tactics and governance were also issues at BYJU'S, but sources fetched only emphasise finance and governance.

### Gaps
- upGrad and Scaler business models (price points, placement-linked fees, EMI/loan financing) not researched in detail.
- PhysicsWallah course prices (e.g., Rs 3,000-5,000 type online batches), YouTube funnel, app features and tech stack not retrieved.
- BYJU'S sales-practice complaints and refund disputes not fetched.

## 4. Study-abroad and college-application counselling (Leverage Edu, Yocket) and "collegeapp"

### Takeaway
Study-abroad counselling mostly earns commissions from universities, not student fees. Leverage Edu gets about 70% of its revenue from university placement commissions and adds loans/forex and test-prep products. Yocket uses a hybrid: free tools, a premium package for students (about $500), and fees from universities.

### Cited Findings
- Leverage Edu FY25: revenue Rs 173 crore (+91%), net loss Rs 106 crore. Student placement (university commissions) Rs 120.6 crore (about 70%); Fly financial services (loans, forex, accommodation) Rs 29.7 crore; product sales (e.g., IELTS prep) Rs 21.2 crore; Rs 51.2 crore paid in agent commissions; 76% of revenue from India. About $70M raised: $6.5M Series A (Feb 2021), $22M Series B (2022), $40M Series C (Jul 2023, led by ETS) — [Indian Startup Times](https://www.indianstartuptimes.com/?p=26312)
- Yocket (YourStory, March 2020): multi-sided model. Students use most services free. Premium help is about $500 per student for end-to-end support. Universities pay service fees, quoted at $10,000-$100,000 a year. Loan/forex providers are add-ons. Claimed 360,000 registered students, 100+ university tie-ups, about $1M revenue at the time. 2020 data — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2020/03/edtech-startup-yocket-career-counselling)
- Leverage Edu has an agent-partner programme and AI-plus-human-agent model (headlines only) — [Leverage partner page](https://leverageedu.com/partnerwithleverageedu/); [Open Magazine](https://openthemagazine.com/business/leverage-edus-ai-bet-runs-through-the-human-agent)
- 10MS offers "free 1:1 study abroad support" bundled with its IELTS Programme, which fits the funnel from test prep to counselling — [10MS IELTS Programme](https://10minuteschool.com/en/product/ielts-programme/)

### Inferences
- "collegeapp" is NOT identified. Dedicated research was not done. Most likely meanings (my guess, no source): a generic term for a college-application and counselling product like Yocket or Leverage Edu, or the Common App. Ask the user to clarify.
- For a Dhaka founder, test prep (IELTS/GRE) leads naturally to counselling. Monetization options are university commissions or recruitment-partner agreements, loan/forex referrals, and paid review packages. Regulatory and trust issues for agent commissions in Bangladesh were not researched.

### Gaps
- "collegeapp" identification: not resolved.
- CollegeVine, Common App, Crimson and BD study-abroad agencies (e.g., commission rates) not researched.
- No data on Bangladeshi students' study-abroad spend or agent fee levels.

## 5. Cross-cutting questions: price points and payments, cheap acquisition, failure modes, tech stack

### Takeaway
Only partial evidence. Price points observed: BDT 5,500-6,000 per skills course, BDT 12,000-24,000 for subscriptions, BDT 9,500-15,000 for offline IELTS. Acquisition via free YouTube/Facebook is confirmed for 10MS (weak source). Known failure modes include low completion, political/funding shocks and, in India, over-leverage.

### Cited Findings
- Price points above: [Mentors'](https://www.mentors.com.bd/course-fee), [10MS brochure](https://cdn.10minuteschool.com/images/Brochure_10MS_English_Centre_1715256964842.pdf), [Bohubrihi](https://futurestartup.com/2023/10/25/bohubrihi-subscription/), [Ostad](https://en.prothomalo.com/youth/education/wlc3y5n7xq), [Programming Hero](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/271810)
- Support channels confirmed on 10MS IELTS pages: Facebook support group, weekly Zoom doubt-solving, 24/7 hotline 16910 — [10MS IELTS Course](https://10minuteschool.com/en/product/ielts-course); [Refund Policy](https://10minuteschool.com/refund-policy)
- Failure modes: 33% completion at Programming Hero; Startup Bangladesh funding withdrawn and 10MS class halt (Aug 2024); BYJU'S debt/governance collapse; Unacademy 94% valuation drop.

### Inferences
- A launch IELTS/GRE price anchored under BDT 10,000 for live/hybrid and lower for recorded would sit below the offline band, but this is untested.
- Mock-test/exam-series products are cheap to deliver and a core part of 10MS and Mentors' IELTS offers.

### Gaps
- Payment methods used by each platform (bKash, Nagad, Rocket, SSLCommerz, cards): not found in any fetched page.
- Tech stack, job postings, BuiltWith data: not found.
- Gamification, user roles (parent, moderator), dashboards: only scattered hints (analytics at Shikho; progress tracking at 10MS).
- Reddit/Facebook complaint threads were not fetched. Student complaints beyond the refund policy are unverified.
