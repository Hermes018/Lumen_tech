## 1. Official/commercial prep materials, prices, and what a platform may do

### Takeaway
Cambridge IELTS 20 (2025) is the latest numbered Cambridge practice-test book found; UK list price for the Academic book with digital pack is about GBP 40.49. IELTS.org content is licensed for personal, non-commercial use only, so a commercial platform should recommend official books as companion resources and never copy their items. BDT prices and Nilkhet details were not found.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Cambridge IELTS 20 is probably the latest number as of Nov 2026 per search results, but a Cambridge IELTS 21 could exist; verify on cambridge.org before publishing "latest edition" claims.
- Safe operating rule: recommend titles by name and link to legitimate sellers (Rokomari etc.); do not reproduce, retype, paraphrase item-by-item, or reuse audio/passages from them; do not use "IELTS" trade marks as if endorsed (use "IELTS-style", with a disclaimer). Trade-mark wording on ielts.org is strict, so get legal advice on nominative use in marketing.
- Piracy: any photocopied/PDF Cambridge or ETS content sold or shared on the platform would be infringement; the platform should have a contributor/teacher warranty and takedown policy. (No source found on Nilkhet; this is general reasoning.)

## 2. Open/licensed text and audio sources

### Takeaway
VOA-produced text/audio/video is public domain, but VOA-licensed third-party (AP, Reuters, AFP) material is not, so VOA Learning English usable only if the specific item is VOA-original. LibriVox audio is dedicated to the public domain. Wikipedia text is CC BY-SA, which has share-alike implications; the safest route remains original passages.

### Cited Findings
- VOA: "All text, audio and video material produced exclusively by the Voice of America is in the public domain"; licensed material (VOA holds licences from AFP, AP, Reuters) is not public domain and may not be copied, redistributed, sold or published without permission; VOA and voanews.com are trademarks that may not be used commercially without permission; credit requested — [VOA copyright page](https://www.voanews.com/p/5338.html)
- LibriVox: "donates its recordings to the public domain" and "All our audio is in the public domain, so you may use it for whatever purpose you wish"; the underlying books are public-domain works — [LibriVox About](https://librivox.org/pages/about-librivox/). LibriVox wiki on copyright and public domain (not read in detail; note US-only PD status of texts) — [LibriVox wiki](https://wiki.librivox.org/index.php/Copyright_and_Public_Domain)
- Creative Commons ShareAlike interpretation (adaptations must be licensed under the same/compatible licence; relevance to combined works) — [CC wiki](https://wiki.creativecommons.org/wiki/ShareAlike_interpretation); CC BY-SA 4.0 deed — [creativecommons.org](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-SA/4.0/.)
- Wikipedia reuse guidance page exists — [Wikipedia:Reusing Wikipedia content mirror](https://wiki.riteme.site/wiki/Wikipedia:Reusing_Wikipedia_content/compliant_examples) (not read in full)

### Inferences
- A passage adapted from Wikipedia text is a CC BY-SA adaptation: must credit and release the adapted passage under CC BY-SA, which is incompatible with a paywalled, all-rights-reserved item bank unless the passage is separately licensable. Writing fresh text from facts (facts are not copyrightable in most jurisdictions) avoids this.
- Project Gutenberg texts are mostly US-public-domain; old-language style differs from IELTS/GRE register, and Gutenberg's licence terms for its trademark were not checked.
- VOA caution: verify each item is VOA-exclusive; keep a provenance log per passage.

### Gaps
- OpenStax (CC BY), UK Open Government Licence v3, Project Gutenberg terms, and AP/Reuters licensing costs: not retrieved.
- Whether VOA Learning English is still operating/published in late 2026 (US agency changes were not checked).
- Bangladesh-law considerations for CC/PD status.

## 4. Listening audio production

### Takeaway
ElevenLabs publishes API and plan prices; Amazon Polly pricing was found via a secondary source; Azure, Google, OpenAI prices and voice-actor rates were not verified.

### Cited Findings
- ElevenLabs API/pricing page: Starter USD 6/month (first month USD 1), Creator USD 22/month (first month USD 11; includes 275,000 TTS characters), Pro USD 99, Scale USD 299, Business USD 990, Enterprise custom; per-usage TTS: v4 and v3 USD 0.08 per 1K characters, v4 Turbo / Flash USD 0.04 per 1K characters; music commercial use requires Starter or higher; prices exclude taxes — [ElevenLabs pricing](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing/api) (fetched summarised 2026-11-07; verify plan-specific commercial-licence terms in the ElevenLabs terms of service)
- Amazon Polly per 1M characters (reviewed 1 Aug 2026, secondary source): Standard USD 4, Neural USD 16, Long-Form USD 100, Generative USD 30 — [offlinetts.com](https://offlinetts.com/blog/tts-pricing-comparison-2026/); official page: [AWS Polly pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/polly/pricing/)
- Third-party aggregators indicate Google Cloud TTS ranges USD 4 to USD 160 per 1M characters — [costbench](https://www.costbench.com/software/ai-voice-tools/google-cloud-text-to-speech/) (secondary, low confidence)

### Inferences
- Rough cost: a 30-minute IELTS-style section is about 4,500 words, about 27,000 characters; at ElevenLabs USD 0.08 per 1K characters that is about USD 2.2 per section per take; Polly Neural about USD 0.43. Budget for regenerations.
- Authentic multi-accent exam audio (British, Australian, NZ, Canadian, North American) is easier from voice actors; TTS fine for practice but disclose it as synthetic.

### Gaps
- Azure AI Speech, Google Cloud, OpenAI TTS official prices and licence/commercial terms; accent coverage per provider; multi-speaker dialogue features.
- Fiverr/Voices.com/Bangladeshi voice-talent rates.
- Recording standards (sample rate, LUFS, noise floor): not sourced (common spoken-word targets are around -16 to -19 LUFS, 44.1/48 kHz, per the brief only).

## 5. Video lesson production

### Takeaway
Udemy requires at least 720p, 16:9, and 30 minutes minimum of video per course; the Guo et al. edX study supports short videos (under 6 minutes ideal).

### Cited Findings
- Udemy Video Standards: HD at least 720p, horizontal 16:9; visuals must relate to narration with dynamic graphics (static image/slide alone is low quality); courses need at least 30 minutes of video to publish — [Udemy support](https://support.udemy.com/hc/en-us/articles/229232767-Video-Standards)
- Guo, Kim and Rubin (2014): 6,902,358 sessions, 862 videos, 4 edX courses (all math/science), 127,839 students; length was the strongest predictor of engagement; median engagement at most 6 minutes regardless of length; videos under 3 minutes had highest engagement; for videos over 9 minutes students often stopped before halfway; recommendations include segmenting into chunks under 6 minutes, showing the instructor's head at well-timed points, informal setting, Khan-style tablet drawings, editing out filler/long pauses — [Guo et al. PDF](https://www.cs.rochester.edu/hci/pubs/pdfs/edX-MOOC-video-production-and-engagement_LAS-2014.pdf)

### Inferences
- Study is from 2012 edX data in STEM; applying to language-test prep is an extrapolation. Consider 6-12 min lessons with 3-6 min micro-clips for drills.

### Gaps
- Udemy audio/lighting guidance, Coursera guidelines, budget kit prices (Rode/Shure/Fifine/Maono), tool pricing (OBS, Descript, CapCut, DaVinci, Camtasia), captions standards, Bangla/English bilingual delivery evidence: not retrieved.

## 6. Band descriptors and scoring guides

### Takeaway
Public band descriptors are published as PDFs on ielts.org, but ielts.org's site-wide terms limit use to personal, non-commercial; quoting them in a commercial course needs permission or a short-quote/fair-dealing analysis. ETS side not researched.

### Cited Findings
- IELTS public Speaking and Writing band descriptor PDFs on ielts.org — [Speaking](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-speaking-band-descriptors.pdf), [Writing](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf)
- Site terms: personal and non-commercial use only; commercial use/republishing/modifying needs written permission — [IELTS copyright statement](https://ielts.org/legal/ielts-copyright-and-trade-mark-statement). The statement as summarised does not mention band descriptors specifically.
- IELTS Writing scales review/update (Clark et al. 2023) — [IELTS research PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Research/ielts-writing-scales-review-and-update-summary-overview-clark-et-al-2023.pdf)

### Inferences
- Safest: link to the official descriptors and write your own rubric in your own words (four criteria names are factual); request written permission via the form if reproducing verbatim.

### Gaps
- ETS scoring guides (GRE Analytical Writing rubric) licensing: not retrieved. British Council/IDP specific permission for descriptors not confirmed.

## 7. Content operations tooling

### Takeaway
QTI 3.0 final release (core ASI features) was published May 2022 and is not backward compatible with QTI 2.x.

### Cited Findings
- QTI 3.0 final: published May 2022 (blog 7 Jun 2022); key changes include transform-free authoring-to-delivery, reworked accessibility, richer HTML5 content model, simpler XML; not backward compatible with 2.x; XSD files publicly available; certification via a validator, free for members; ETS-built XSLT migration tool exists but is not formally supported; example QTI packages downloadable — [1EdTech blog](https://www.1edtech.org/blog/finally-final-the-qti-30-release); overview [1EdTech QTI](https://1edtech.org/standards/qti/index), [Beginner's Guide](https://www.imsglobal.org/spec/qti/v3p0/guide)

### Inferences
- For a small platform: keep items in a structured store (spreadsheet/DB) with metadata (skill, band/difficulty, source/provenance, reviewer, status, item statistics), and export to QTI 3.0 only if interop with an LMS is needed.

### Gaps
- Tool support in practice (open-source QTI 3 editors/importers), review workflow tools, and later-than-2022 QTI updates: not researched.
