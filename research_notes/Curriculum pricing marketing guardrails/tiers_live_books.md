# Tier structures, live classes, human-feedback pricing, printed books, promotions and completion benchmarks (as of 2026-11-07)

Coverage note: about 17 tool calls were used. Many pricing pages are JS-rendered, so fetches returned no prices. Several platforms in the brief (Kaplan, Princeton Review, TTP, IDP, IELTS Advantage, Mentors', Saifurs, Bohubrihi, Leverage IELTS, Yocket, upGrad Abroad, Manhattan Prep) were NOT verified and are listed under Gaps.

## 1. Tier structures and perks

### Takeaway
Observed ladders run from Free (1 AI-scored essay or test) through cheap monthly AI-only passes ($6-15) to premium self-study with a score guarantee ($109-219), and then to live or human-reviewed tiers. At the low end, human feedback is rarely bundled; where it exists it is limited to a few expert-marked items or private tutorials per package. BD leaders (10 Minute School) sell a recorded course with a free hardcopy book and weekly Zoom doubt classes, plus a separate live batch.

### Cited Findings
- Magoosh GRE Premium: 1 month $149, 6 months $179 (or 4x$44.75), and 6 months + 12 months admissions tools $219. Perks: official ETS questions, 290+ videos, 1,600+ practice questions, up to 6 practice tests, score predictor. All GRE plans carry a +5-point score guarantee (money back if not met). Secondary source (bloggersideas review, 2026), not Magoosh's own page, so verify. — [bloggersideas Magoosh pricing 2026](https://www.bloggersideas.com/?p=2163343)
- Magoosh IELTS Premium: 1 month $109, 6 months $129 (or 4x$16.13). Perks: 850+ questions, 120+ videos, up to 10 full mocks, score predictor, unlimited AI speaking and writing grading, and a target band guarantee with terms not specified. No human grading mentioned. Same secondary source. — [bloggersideas](https://www.bloggersideas.com/?p=2163343)
- Note the pricing mechanic: the 6-month plan is only $20-30 above the 1-month plan, which nudges buyers to the longer plan. — [bloggersideas](https://www.bloggersideas.com/?p=2163343)
- IELTS.international (AI platform, Bangla page): Free = score 1 essay. Monthly Pass $5.99 = 20 AI-scored essays, 8 speaking sessions of 15 min, 40 reading/listening sets, 30 days. Exam Pass $15 for an 8-week one-time purchase (no renewal). Feedback is AI-only and described as approximate. A 14-day refund window applies to first purchases. Human tutors are mentioned only as a separate per-hour option. — [ielts.international/bn/pricing](https://www.ielts.international/bn/pricing)
- PrepEdu IELTS Lab: Free (1 speaking + 1 writing AI test per month), Core (8 speaking + 8 writing per month, credits reset monthly with no rollover), Premium (unlimited practice + full video course with an AI tutor). Prices did not render. Cancel anytime, no auto-renew. AI is trained with input from IELTS teachers and a former examiner. — [prepedu pricing](https://prepedu.com/en/ielts/lab/pricing)
- E2Language (Australia): features are not tied to named packages in the page text. They include 45-minute private lessons with an expert teacher ("1, 2 or 3 tutorials per package"), expert marking of practice exams by ex-examiner-designed questions with feedback within 48 hours, and live interactive classes in multiple time zones with recordings. 14-day free trial with no card. Diwali promo: 25% off upgrades, every 5th order 50% off, code LIGHT25, until 11 Nov 2026. Base prices not retrieved. — [E2Language IELTS Academic](https://www.e2language.com/BundlePackage/IELTS/Academic)
- 10 Minute School IELTS Course (recorded): about 50 hrs, 54 videos, 38 lecture sheets, 10 Reading + 10 Listening mocks, lifetime validity, one free hardcopy book (*ঘরে বসে IELTS প্রস্তুতি*, Munzereen Shahid) delivered to students, weekly Zoom doubt-solving, Facebook support group, 34,750 enrollments claimed. Price not shown on the page (explained via a video). — [10MS IELTS course](https://10minuteschool.com/en/product/ielts-course)
- 10 Minute School IELTS Live Batch: 12 weeks of study with 12 months validity, 36 live classes (50 min lecture + 30 min Q&A), 8 Reading + 8 Listening practice tests, 2 full mocks, 10 full mock questions for home practice, "expert feedback" on speaking and writing strategies, 1:1 mentor consultation sessions, and an exclusive IELTS book. Price not shown. — [10MS IELTS live batch](https://10minuteschool.com/en/product/ielts-live-batch)
- British Council India online IELTS prep class: INR 4,750 per course. Max 20 students; 12 hrs live (1x3h or 2x1.5h per week) + 30 hrs self-paced over 4 weeks; 2 essays with feedback; speaking feedback in class. — [British Council FAQ](https://www.britishcouncil.in/english/online/classes/ielts-preparation/faqs)
- PhysicsWallah (PW) runs IELTS batches ("IELTS Expert", evening 9:00-10:30 PM batches, "Zero to Hero"), which confirms evening 90-minute live slots, but the pages returned no price or perk details. — [PW IELTS batches](https://www.pw.live/study-abroad/ielts/batches)

### Inferences
- A defensible ladder from these benchmarks:
  - Free: 1 AI essay/test, plus some free R/L.
  - Self-paced: recorded course, book and mocks (10MS pattern).
  - Plus with AI: monthly AI credits (about $6/month or about BDT 700, per IELTS.international and PrepEdu), with credit resets that create monthly usage habits.
  - Pro with live: live batch (10MS pattern).
  - Premium: 1:1 mentor/tutorial slots (E2Language, 10MS).
- Most AI-first competitors explicitly do not offer human grading, so human review is a clear differentiator for upper tiers.
- Score guarantees are used at the $100+ price point (Magoosh), not at cheap tiers.

### Gaps
- No verified data on Manhattan Prep, Kaplan, Princeton Review, TTP, IDP, IELTS Advantage, Mentors', Saifurs, Bohubrihi, Leverage IELTS, Yocket or upGrad Abroad (not reached). PW pricing is unverified.
- PTE, DET, TOEFL and Spoken English tier benchmarks were not researched.
- 10MS prices in BDT are not retrievable from the page text; check manually or in the 10MS app/Facebook.
- Magoosh figures come from a review blog and may be stale; check magoosh.com directly.

## 2. Live class formats

### Takeaway
Verified formats: BC India caps classes at 20, 12 live hours as 1x3h or 2x1.5h per week, not recorded; 10MS runs about 3x/week, 50 min lecture + 30 min Q&A, over 12 weeks; PW uses 9-10:30 PM evening slots.

### Cited Findings
- BC India: max 20 per class; 12 hrs live + 30 hrs guided self-paced activities in 4 weeks (a crash-course-like length); classes are deliberately NOT recorded for privacy, and a student book lets learners catch up; teachers have first degrees plus international English-teaching and online-teaching qualifications, some with DELTA/CELTYL/Master's. — [British Council FAQ](https://www.britishcouncil.in/english/online/classes/ielts-preparation/faqs)
- 10MS live batch: 36 live classes over 12 weeks (about 3/week is my arithmetic, not stated), each 50 min teaching + 30 min Q&A. — [10MS live batch](https://10minuteschool.com/en/product/ielts-live-batch)
- 10MS recorded course adds weekly Zoom doubt-solving and a Facebook group. — [10MS IELTS course](https://10minuteschool.com/en/product/ielts-course)
- PW IELTS "Expert" batches are scheduled 9:00-10:30 PM (90 min evening slots). — [PW batch listing](https://www.pw.live/study-abroad/ielts/batches)
- E2Language offers live classes across multiple time zones with recordings. — [E2Language](https://www.e2language.com/BundlePackage/IELTS/Academic)

### Inferences
- A 90-minute evening class with a 2-3x/week cadence is standard for working adults.
- Recording policy varies (BC no, E2Language yes), so it is a design choice. Offering recordings to paid live tiers helps catch-up but may reduce live attendance.

### Gaps
- No data found on TA/moderator ratios, attendance benchmarks, homework systems, speaking-club formats, mock cadence in live courses, or platform choice beyond Zoom (10MS). Crash-course specifics (2-4 weeks) are covered only by the BC 4-week format.

## 3. Human feedback pricing

### Takeaway
No reliable per-essay or per-speaking-mock prices were found. What was found: human review is bundled in limited counts (BC: 2 essays within a INR 4,750 course; E2Language: expert marking with 48-hour turnaround, 1-3 private 45-min tutorials per package).

### Cited Findings
- E2Language: expert marking with feedback within 48 hours; questions designed by ex-examiners; 45-min private lessons. — [E2Language](https://www.e2language.com/BundlePackage/IELTS/Academic)
- BC India: 2 essays with feedback included in the course price. — [British Council FAQ](https://www.britishcouncil.in/english/online/classes/ielts-preparation/faqs)
- IELTS.international prices AI feedback at roughly $0.30 per essay inside its pass (20 essays for $5.99; my arithmetic, which also bundles speaking and R/L), and treats private tutors as a separate per-hour market. — [ielts.international](https://www.ielts.international/bn/pricing)

### Inferences
- Human feedback is roughly 10-50x more costly than AI feedback per item, so it should be capped by tier (counts per month) rather than unlimited. The multiple is my inference, not sourced.

### Gaps
- No USD or BDT per-review prices, BD freelancer examiner rates or examiner qualification norms were found (beyond BC teacher qualifications above).

## 5. Discount and promotion mechanics

### Takeaway
Seen mechanics: seasonal promo codes with escalating loyalty (E2Language Diwali), installment payments (Magoosh), score guarantees, free trials and short refund windows.

### Cited Findings
- E2Language: 25% off upgrades, every 5th order 50% off, code LIGHT25, ends 11 Nov 2026; 14-day free trial, no card. — [E2Language](https://www.e2language.com/BundlePackage/IELTS/Academic)
- Magoosh: 4 installment payments on 6-month plans; GRE +5-point guarantee; IELTS target-band guarantee; extension "usually" granted by email if the test date moves. — [bloggersideas](https://www.bloggersideas.com/?p=2163343)
- IELTS.international: 14-day refund on first purchase; cancel before renewal. — [ielts.international](https://www.ielts.international/bn/pricing)
- PrepEdu: no auto-renew, cancel anytime. — [prepedu](https://prepedu.com/en/ielts/lab/pricing)

### Inferences
- Guarantee abuse controls (not found in sources) are typically completion/usage conditions; this was not verified. Design them yourself.

### Gaps
- No terms for guarantees' abuse controls, referral credits, group discounts or scholarship mechanics were found.

## 6. Completion benchmarks

### Takeaway
Free MOOCs complete at about 3-6%; paid verified learners complete at 44-56%. The relationship is correlational (selection effect), so paid commitment predicts but may not cause completion.

### Cited Findings
- Completion of all participants: 4.96% (2013-14), 5.91%, 4.13%, 3.98%, 3.13% (2017-18). Among learners who intended to complete: 11.51% to 15.42-17.74%. Among verified (paid) learners: 44.23%, 50.17%, 49.73%, 56.43%, 46.02%. 52% of registrants never enter the courseware. Authors say completion has not meaningfully improved over six years. — [Reich & Ruipérez-Valiente, Science 2019 postprint](https://webs.um.es/jruiperez/papers/journals/2019_Science_MOOCPivot_postprint.pdf)
- Caveat noted in the source: the text's 7% second-year retention figure conflicts with Table S1's 9.98% for one cohort. — same source

### Inferences
- Plan for roughly 45-55% completion among paid learners at best, and lower for cheap tiers. Cohorts, deadlines and accountability are standard levers, but no source in this set quantified their effect.

### Gaps
- No benchmarks for paid test-prep completion specifically, nor quantified effects of cohorts, deadlines or accountability.
