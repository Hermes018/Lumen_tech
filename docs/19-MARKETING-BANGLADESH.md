## 1. Market reality check and strategy

### 1.1 What the evidence says (and what it does not)

| Signal | Fact | Implication for marketing |
|---|---|---|
| Test volume is falling | IDP Language Testing volume −8% in FY26 (to 30 Jun 2026); India −50% FY25 and −27% H1 FY26; IDP's FY27 planning assumes a **20–30% fall** in market volume vs FY26; IDP cut IELTS venues from ~1,500 to under 600 `[src: marketing_international_gapfill.md §A1 — IDP FY26 release]`. No Bangladesh-specific test-taker count exists in our notes. | Do not plan on a growing "IELTS for study" pool. Plan on a smaller, more serious, more price-sensitive buyer, and on share-of-wallet across several exams. |
| Visa shocks | US F-1: 73% of Bangladeshi applicants refused (2025), new BD students −69% (Sept 2025 vs 2024); Australia: 51% of offshore BD applicants refused (Feb 2026); several UK universities halted BD admissions (headlines) `[src: bangladesh_market_gapfill.md §7 — ICEF Monitor, June 2026]`. | GRE (US-heavy) is a secondary line. | Canada | CLB 7 = IELTS General Training 6.0 in each skill; IELTS Academic is **not** accepted for Express Entry; CELPIP and PTE Core also accepted `[src: marketing_international_gapfill.md §B8 — Immigration2Canada (third party, VERIFY on IRCC)]`. | A test-agnostic "Canada track" aimed at 30–50-year-olds. Paid media is for retargeting and lookalikes; the base is organic and partner-led (≥60%). |

**Honest summary.** The Bangladesh study-abroad test-prep market is large in absolute terms (60,000–70,000 outbound students a year, undated order-of-magnitude, **weak**) but is contracting and policy-shocked. The enduring need is "English for a better life" across study, migration, jobs and professional registration. That is the strategy: a **portfolio**, with the free funnel and the trust brand shared across every line.

### 1.3 Positioning statement

**English.** For Bangladeshi learners who need English or an international test for study, work or migration, Lumen Academy is the Bangla-and-English learning platform that lets you practise on exam-faithful screens, get an honest estimated score with clear feedback, and learn at a price that respects your budget. Unlike coaching centres and agents, we guarantee no band and no visa; we guarantee clear practice, plain pricing and a straight answer to "which test do you actually need?"

**বাংলা।** যাঁরা পড়াশোনা, চাকরি বা অভিবাসনের জন্য ইংরেজি বা আন্তর্জাতিক পরীক্ষার প্রস্তুতি নিচ্ছেন, তাঁদের জন্য Lumen Academy এমন একটি প্ল্যাটফর্ম যেখানে বাংলায় বুঝে, আসল পরীক্ষার মতো স্ক্রিনে অনুশীলন করে সৎ ও স্পষ্ট ফিডব্যাক পাওয়া যায়, আপনার সাধ্যের মধ্যে। আমরা ব্যান্ড স্কোর বা ভিসার নিশ্চয়তা দিই না; আমরা দিই সঠিক অনুশীলন, স্বচ্ছ মূল্য আর "আপনার আসলে কোন পরীক্ষা দরকার" প্রশ্নের সোজা উত্তর।

**Brand promise (one line).**
- EN: "Practise like it's the real exam. Know where you stand. Pay only for what you need."
- বাংলা: "আসল পরীক্ষার মতো অনুশীলন। নিজের অবস্থান জানুন। যা দরকার, শুধু তার জন্যই খরচ করুন।"

### 1.4 Honesty pillars (non-negotiable, repeated in §6 and §8)

1. **No guaranteed band, score or visa.** Every score we show is "estimated" (D6). বাংলা: "আনুমানিক স্কোর, অফিসিয়াল নয়।"
2. **Tell people when they do not need us.** The "which test do you need?" quiz will sometimes send a user to free official materials (e.g. HRD Korea EPS-TOPIK booklets). This costs a few sales and buys the trust that the fraud environment rewards.
3. **Anti-fraud by design.** Certificate verification page (`/verify/[code]`), an "official channels only" page, no agent payments through Lumen, no question "leaks", original content only (D6).
4. **Transparent pricing.** One price, VAT-inclusive, no fake "was" prices (02 §7.1). Competitor listings with crossed-out prices and expiry dates exist `[src: bd_segments_marketing.md §3 — Mentors]`; we differentiate by not doing it. Founding-member price locks are real and capped (§4.4).
5. **Consent-based marketing.** Explicit opt-in per channel, no under-18 ad targeting (D7, PDPO).

## 6. Trust and safety marketing

### 6.1 Transparent AI-scoring accuracy
- Publish a **methodology + validation page** at L2 (02 §9.3): sample size (≥ 200 essays, 02 §14), agreement within 0.5 band vs certified examiners (target ≥ 80%; competitors self-report 70–79%), error cases, what the AI cannot judge. Update quarterly.
- Label every score "estimated". Show confidence ranges and the reasons. Allow a request for a human grader review (D10 tiers).
- Do not compare against "official" scoring; do not use "examiner-level".
- Respond to criticism publicly. IDP warns against LLM grading `[src: 02 §1]`; our answer is evidence, not adjectives.

### 6.2 Teacher credentials verification
- "Verified teacher" badge only after: government ID check, certificate/score report verification (with issuing body where possible), a trial lesson, a conflict-of-interest declaration, two-factor (D4).
- Public teacher pages show what was verified and when. Do not claim "ex-examiner" without proof (r/IELTS-style bans exist for fake claims `[src: marketing_international_gapfill.md §A2]`).

### 6.3 Refund and pricing clarity
- One page, plain Bangla and English: **7 days if < 20% consumed; no refund on consumed AI credits; certificates revoked on refund** (D5/02 §7.1). Compare: 10MS 48-hour window and 7–14 working-day refunds `[src: south_asia_edtech.md §1]`; state our refund timeline in the page.
- Show refund terms at checkout, on every product page, and in the receipt (Digital Commerce Guidelines 2021 require displaying purchase/return conditions `[src: Daily Star overview]`).

### 6.4 Testimonials and results policy
- Written, revocable consent; real names optional; no payment for the *content* of a review (a thank-you token only if disclosed); no editing that changes meaning; **score claims only with a verified score report** (shown with consent); always show the baseline, time spent, practice done and "individual result, not typical".
- Never publish fake or fabricated reviews, AI-generated testimonials, or purchased ratings. Legal compliance includes the Digital Commerce Guidelines (platforms cannot erase ratings/reviews `[src: Daily Star overview]`); also false advertising under the Consumer Rights Protection Act 2009 (**VERIFY with lawyer**, research gap).
- Disclose: no outcomes promised, results vary.

### 6.5 Anti-scam content and brand protection
- "Official channels" page and pinned post; we never ask for OTP, bKash PIN or cash.
- Expect impersonation (fake Lumen pages/WhatsApp numbers). Process: weekly brand-monitoring search, report-and-takedown on Meta/Google/YouTube, public warning, SMS/WhatsApp line "only these numbers".
- Content series on scams (see 2.d) and a public **"Report a scam" form** forwarded to the relevant authority (VERIFY which).
- Partners (UDC entrepreneurs, ambassadors, coaching centres) sign a code of conduct; violations lead to termination and public removal from the partner list.

### 6.6 Common App, admissions and visa honesty
- No admissions or scholarship guarantee; no ghost-writing of essays (coaching only); Common App fraud and integrity rules apply to any service (VERIFY current policy). No sharing of student data with universities, lenders or agents without explicit consent (PDPO, 02 §7.4).
- Commission disclosure for any referral; no recommendation by payout (02 §7.4).
- Visa: general information with official sources; "we are not an immigration consultancy". No under-18 referrals (02 §7.4).

## 8. Compliance

### 8.1 Advertising claims (pre-publication checklist)
Never: "guaranteed band/score/visa/admission/job"; "100% pass"; "certificate without test"; "leaked questions"; "official" or "approved by" an exam body; unverified rankings ("No. 1"); fake countdown timers; fake "was" prices; "limited seats" unless the limit is real; testimonials without consent; implying that the Probashi Kallyan Bank loan pays for our courses.
Always: "estimated" on every score; price VAT-inclusive; refund terms visible; "results vary"; the trademark disclaimer where a test mark appears (below); **who we are** (legal name, trade licence/BIN, contact) in the footer; paid creator disclosures.
Process: every creative passes the checklist (academic lead + growth lead) before publishing; a lawyer reviews the checklist template once.

### 8.2 Digital Commerce Operation Guidelines 2021
Display price, charges and purchase/return conditions; delivery within **5 days (same city) / 10 days (other city)** for printed books; do not erase ratings or reviews; the authority can cancel trade licence/VAT registration; complaints go to the Directorate of Consumer Rights Protection `[src: bd_segments_marketing.md §5 — Daily Star overview]`. Refund rules were not in the summary (VERIFY). Consumer Rights Protection Act 2009 advertising provisions: **not researched**, lawyer to confirm.

### 8.3 Data protection and consent (PDPO 2025)
Per 02 §12.3: consent must be informed, explicit, specific and withdrawable; parental consent for children; **profiling, behavioural tracking and targeted ads aimed at minors prohibited**; fines up to 5% of annual turnover; commencement date unconfirmed; localisation question unresolved. Marketing design:
- Separate, unticked consent boxes for email, SMS, WhatsApp, and personalised advertising; consent logs (who, what, when, version).
- Meta/Google pixels and server events only after consent (cookie banner, PostHog gating, D3).
- Lead ads: consent copy in Bangla and English, DOB gate; delete leads that never consented.
- Withdrawal link in every message; honour within 72 hours (A-MKT19).
- Partners (UDC, ambassadors) collecting data: they use the Lumen form/OTP flow, not WhatsApp spreadsheets.
- Data processors (Meta, WhatsApp BSP, SMS aggregator): data-transfer assessment (02 §12.3); VERIFY the "equivalent protection" test.

### 8.4 Minors
No ad targeting of under-18s; organic content only; guardian-consent flow; no referrals or lead capture below 18 without a guardian; age bands self-declared (D7) but advertising platforms' age controls are not sufficient proof of age; admissions services for minors require a guardian contract.

### 8.5 Test marks in ads (ETS, IELTS, PTE, DET, OET)
- **ETS (GRE/TOEFL)**: required notice "[mark] is a registered trademark of ETS. This product is not endorsed or approved by ETS."; no ETS marks in company/product/domain/handles/trademark filings/**ad keywords**; no logos; no plural/possessive full test names; submit materials to ETS (about 10 business days) `[src: 02 §9.4/§12.2 — exam_specifications_gapfill.md §6]`.
- **IELTS, PTE, DET, OET**: policies not retrieved; assume equivalent; IELTS is owned by the British Council, IDP and Cambridge University Press & Assessment (background). **Take a lawyer's view before launch (VERIFY).** Practical default: use the exam name as a descriptive topic word ("IELTS preparation") in content titles only if the lawyer clears it; avoid exam marks as paid-search keywords until cleared; do not use exam logos; keep brand "Lumen Academy" neutral (trademark search needed, 02 §12.2).
- Meta/Google ad policies on trademarks and education: **VERIFY**.

### 8.6 SMS/WhatsApp
BTRC and aggregator rules (§3.8): VERIFY. WhatsApp/Meta messaging policy compliance. Bots disclose automation.

### 8.7 Recruitment and immigration
Lumen is a training provider; do not advertise foreign jobs; do not collect payments for placement; do not give individual immigration advice (non-lawyers); partners are vetted (§2.f). Overseas employment licensing rules: **not researched**, lawyer to confirm.
